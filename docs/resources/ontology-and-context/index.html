What an ontology knows. What context remembers.

Structure and explanation serve different purposes.

On this pageWhat existsWhy it happenedUse both without replacing eitherKeep boundaries explicit

What exists

An ontology defines business objects, links, actions, logic, security and ownership. P-104 can be a business item connected to its order and dispatch records.

Why it happened

Context preserves explanations, corrections, decisions and exceptions. A note about a separate dispatch explains a quantity difference but must remain attached to the relevant evidence.

Use both without replacing either

The explanation does not replace the original record or the object model. Together, structured relationships and retained reasons support a more useful business understanding.

Keep boundaries explicit

Context is not an unrestricted memory. Access, source references and ownership need to follow the work. The fictional example illustrates the relationship rather than executing it.
