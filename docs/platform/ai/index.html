Architecture

More than a chat window.

A business agent needs objects, context, tools and a controlled place to act. Steed is the employee-facing product on that foundation.

AI architecture / conceptual path
  1. Employee request

    What needs attention?

  2. Business understanding

    Objects, context and evidence

  3. Tool or proposed action

    Applicable access and authorization

  4. Execution record

    Keep responsibility visible

Illustrates responsibilities; no model call or business command is executed.

Understand

Relate a request to the business objects and explanations behind it. A natural-language interface is the beginning, not the whole system.

Use tools

Separate a model’s suggestion from a permitted business command. The authority to read and the authority to change are different.

Keep responsibility visible

Define the person or agent’s scope, the action path and the record. Model choices and exact assistant surfaces are confirmed with the deployment.
