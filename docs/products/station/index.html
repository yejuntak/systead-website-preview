Station

A place for your business AI to work.

Bring compute, storage, networking and equipment connections into one on-site operating foundation.

The environment
is part of the product.

AI and business software depend on compute, storage, networking and equipment interfaces. Station gives those dependencies a place in the operating design.

Keep resources
close to the data.

GDDP brings storage, computation and inference close to business information. Station is the runtime foundation; permitted external processing remains an explicit boundary decision.
